Paste your app's address. We read it and say what people come there to do. You can correct that line. Then five test visitors try that job in a real browser, three times each. You see who completed it, who got stuck, and the screen where it happened.

Each test visitor is driven by an NVIDIA Nemotron model on Nebius Token Factory. They only read. They do not sign in, type, buy or save anything. They are not people.

These visitors are software, not people. They only read. Sign-in and saving are not tested.

A full run takes about a minute. The same task on the same address reopens the saved run at no cost.
